## 1. What this fixture is

`fixtures/milp_v1_solved_example.json` is a solved example of the MILP output
contract v1.0. It is generated by `build_fixture.py` from the frozen contract
template at commit `ef29b89`, which I checked byte-for-byte against the copy
Yousef validated in `AI/results/MILP_Output_Validation_Notes.md`.

The solved values are not from a real solve. They reproduce the solution already
stored in the Sprint 2 toy fixture
(`AI/explanations/llm_reporting/fixtures/model_output_example.json`), so the same
allocation now exists in both schemas. That gives us a way to test the adapter:
both fixtures should adapt to the same internal result.

The fixture satisfies the contract's own consistency checks (§9): total cost
reconciles to `solver.objective_value` at 184150.0, withdrawals equal
source-to-plant flows equal delivered volume at 500 ML/day, and all three quality
parameters sit within their model-space limits.

## 3. The pH margin problem

The safety margin for pH is 23.78% computed in model space and 30.5% computed in
pH space. Same water, same limits, same formula. The gap is the log transform:
v1.0 stores pH as hydrogen-ion concentration, and that scale is non-linear and
runs in the opposite direction to pH.

Alkalinity is the tightest parameter either way at 22.55%, so the reported
minimum margin is unchanged for this scenario and nothing looks wrong today.

That is exactly why it needs writing down. The error is invisible until pH happens
to be the tightest parameter, and at that point it surfaces as a margin figure
that is too generous by several points. It would read as a data problem rather
than a units problem, and we would go looking in the wrong place.

The rule for the harness: quality margins are computed from `model_value` against
`model_min` / `model_max`. `reported_value` is for display only and must never be
used in a calculation.

## 4. Values that could not be populated

Two fields are deliberately left null rather than filled with a guess.

`sources[].utilisation_percent` — utilisation is withdrawal against the source's
withdrawal bound, and v1.0 no longer echoes those bounds. They are not in the
scenario files either; they come from the Supabase source view.

`groundwater_bore_1.decision_evidence.unit_cost_rank` — its `cost_per_ml` is not
published in the toy fixture or the scenario file, so it cannot be ranked against
the other two sources.

Both have the same cause and the same consequence for Task 59: the harness cannot
work from a results file alone. It needs the matching ScenarioData or
ModelParameters passed alongside, or these values stay null and the KPI
calculator loses the bounds it needs.

## 5. Suspected defect in the Sprint 2 toy fixture

The toy fixture reports `source_capacity_silvan_reservoir` with a slack of 40371
against a link capacity of 350 ML/day. A slack two orders of magnitude larger than
the capacity it belongs to looks like a storage-capacity value leaking into a
daily-flow field.

I am flagging this rather than fixing it. The toy fixture belongs to the Task 21
work, not Task 59, and changing it would move the goalposts for anyone currently
testing against it.
